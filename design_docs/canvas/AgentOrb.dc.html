Anatomy & rules
Membrane. A translucent sphere with a Fresnel rim: near-clear at the centre, bright at the edge, a specular highlight top-left and an outer halo. Everything inside is clipped to it.
Nucleus. Dense, slightly off-centre, speckled with chromatin, inside its own thin envelope. It breathes slowly at rest and pumps when the agent works.
Internals. Organelles drift on their own loops; three tilted rings form a cage, each carrying a bright particle; when running, particles emit from the nucleus to the membrane.
Colour. One token per state (--agent-idle … --agent-raw). Membrane, nucleus, rings and halo are all mixed from it, so a theme swap or a user theme restyles the cell for free.
Motion budget. Opacity, transform and one registered angle property only; each layer composites on the GPU. Honour prefers-reduced-motion: freeze the drift and rings, keep colour and the needs-you ring.
Truthfulness. Reading, running and wrote hold for a minimum beat so a 40 ms query still registers. Needs-you persists until answered. The raw-access tint never animates away.
